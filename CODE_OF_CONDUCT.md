# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project space.
Contributors must communicate professionally, critique ideas rather than people,
respect privacy, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or abusive
behavior.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issues, project
events, and public representation of the ZhiXing community.
